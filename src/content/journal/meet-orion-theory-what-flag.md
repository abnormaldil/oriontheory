Orion Theory is an independent app and game publisher with a simple goal: make mobile experiences that are clear, thoughtful, and enjoyable to return to.

Our first planned game is **What Flag**, a mobile game built around recognizing the flags of countries around the world. The idea is straightforward: look at a flag, make your guess, and discover how much you know.

We’re still preparing the game for release, so we’re not sharing a launch date just yet. As development moves forward, this website will be the place to find updates, product information, and links to our published apps.

We’re starting with one game, but Orion Theory is intended to grow into a home for more mobile apps and games. Each release will have its own page here, along with the information players need to find, use, and get support for it.

Thanks for stopping by. We hope you’ll come back as What Flag gets closer to release.
