## 2. Information we may handle

The information involved depends on the website or product you use. Depending on the feature, it may include:

- **Information you provide:** for example, when you contact us by email or submit a form.
- **Website and technical information:** information needed to deliver and secure the website, which may be processed by our hosting or infrastructure providers.
- **Product information:** app settings, saved progress, preferences, or other information stored or processed by a particular app.
- **Third-party service information:** information handled by advertising, payment, analytics, crash-reporting, or platform services integrated into a product.

We do not claim that every Orion Theory product collects all of these categories. The applicable product's privacy notice describes its actual features and data flows.

## 3. How information is used

Where applicable, information may be used to:

- provide, operate, and maintain a website, app, or game;
- save preferences or product progress;
- respond to questions and support requests;
- process purchases through the relevant platform;
- deliver advertising or rewards where a product includes ads;
- maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues;
- meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information to data brokers. Third-party services may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

## 4. Third-party services

Our products may use services operated by third parties, such as app stores, advertising providers, hosting providers, or payment processors. Those providers may receive or process information as described in the relevant product notice and their own policies.

Examples include Google Play and Google services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy terms. Review the product-specific notice to identify which services are used by that product.

## 5. Storage, retention, and deletion

The storage and retention period depends on the information and the product. Information stored only on your device may be removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it, subject to operating-system backup or restore features. Information held by a third-party provider is subject to that provider's retention and deletion practices.

If you contact us, we will assess your request and respond as appropriate. We may need to retain certain information where required by law or for legitimate security or recordkeeping purposes.

## 6. Children's privacy

Our products may have different intended audiences. We do not use this studio-level notice to make a blanket statement that every product is designed for children or that every product has the same age-related settings.

A product intended for children, or a product available to children, should have an appropriate product-specific notice and comply with applicable platform and legal requirements. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions about a product.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the information and the services we operate. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The security practices of third-party platforms and providers are also governed by their own policies.

## 8. International processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, those providers' terms and safeguards govern their processing. Product-specific notices may provide further details.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control personal information. You may also be able to manage permissions, advertising choices, notifications, or purchases through your device or platform settings.

Contact us using the details above to ask about a privacy request. We may ask for information reasonably needed to identify and handle the request.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our studio, website, or products change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date. A product-specific notice may also be updated when that product's data practices change.
